Live Interview Notes - Organised and Themed Analysis
Interview Summary

The interview highlighted significant challenges with the current desk booking process, which is impacting employee morale, collaboration, productivity, and the overall hybrid working experience. While business targets are still being achieved, employees are experiencing frustration and reduced workplace engagement.

Theme 1: Desk Booking Challenges
Current Problems
Employees previously had no reliable way to book desks on office days.
Attempts to encourage office attendance were undermined by the inability to guarantee workspace availability.
Staff often commuted to the office only to discover there were no desks available.
The existing spreadsheet-based booking system is slow and difficult to use.
Desk availability cannot be confirmed quickly or confidently.
Key Quotes

"Hope and a prayer to get a desk." - Safia

"Commute so far and then end up with no desk." - Safia

Business Impact
All teams are affected.
Meetings are delayed due to uncertainty around working locations.
Some team members attend in person while others join remotely, reducing effectiveness.
Employee morale and engagement are negatively affected.
Client work can experience delays due to coordination issues.
Although there is currently no major impact on business performance, the issue is becoming increasingly frustrating for employees.
Theme 2: Scale of the Problem & Current Workarounds
Scope
The issue affects multiple teams across the organisation.
The company has approximately 80 desks for 200 employees.
Existing Workarounds

Employees have developed informal workarounds, but these do not resolve the underlying problem.

Examples
Staff rely on colleagues who live closer to the office to check desk availability on their behalf.
Employees spend time managing desk bookings instead of focusing on their primary responsibilities.
Key Quote

"Rely on closer people to check these desk bookings, reducing productivity for their actual work."

Impact
Reduced productivity.
Inefficient use of employee time.
Increased dependence on informal processes.
Theme 3: Impact on Collaboration and Company Culture
Hybrid Working Challenges

The interview identified concerns around the long-term impact of hybrid working on collaboration and workplace culture.

Key Issues
Greater reliance on online communication platforms such as Microsoft Teams.
Increased risk of distraction when working remotely.
Reduced opportunities for spontaneous collaboration.
Collaboration Concerns
Discovery sessions and whiteboarding activities are considered important contributors to business success.
Hybrid working has reduced opportunities for these collaborative activities.
New employees find it more difficult to integrate into the company culture.
Relationship building between colleagues has become more challenging.
Key Quote

"Discovery and whiteboarding is a key success. Hybrid has reduced this method."

Positive Observation
Work continues to be completed successfully.
Employees remain productive.
Key Concern

"It's the human element we value."

Theme 4: Issues with the Existing Booking Process
Current Process
Desk bookings are managed through an Excel spreadsheet.
Initially, anyone could modify the spreadsheet and change bookings.
Problems Identified
Employees could alter bookings made by other people.
Lack of accountability and control.
Poor visibility over booking ownership.
Attempted Solution

The process was changed so that two designated employees managed all bookings.

New Issues Created
Employees must contact booking administrators to secure a desk.
Additional administrative workload.
Delays in booking confirmation.
Fairness Concerns
Lack of transparency around booking allocation.
Perceived unfairness in desk allocation.
Double Bookings
Double bookings occur regularly.
These are usually only discovered when employees arrive at the office.
Resolution depends on whether another desk happens to be available.
Theme 5: Employee Wellbeing and Morale
Survey Findings

A recent Gallup poll revealed significant concerns regarding employee wellbeing.

Key Finding
Employee morale is reported to be at its lowest level ever.
Notable Observation

Despite:

Meeting all work targets.
Achieving shareholder targets.
Meeting overall business objectives.

Employee wellbeing and morale remain significantly lower than expected.

Resource Constraints
There is currently insufficient funding and resources to continue supporting the existing manual process.
Last-Minute Booking Challenges
Desk allocation operates largely on a first-come, first-served basis.
Employees with urgent attendance requirements may struggle to secure a workspace.
They must negotiate directly with existing desk holders to arrange changes.
Stakeholder Requirements (COO Priorities)
Usability
The system must be extremely user-friendly.
Users should be able to understand how to book a desk with minimal training.
Navigation and workflows should be simple and intuitive.
Speed & Performance
The system must load quickly.
Desk availability should be visible within seconds.
Performance must support a smooth user experience.
Compliance & Data Management
The solution must comply with GDPR requirements.
Employee data must be handled responsibly and securely.
Only essential information should be collected.
Data should align with workplace booking requirements and no unnecessary data should be stored.
Delivery Constraints
The solution must be delivered within the required project timeline.
Business Focus
Maximise efficiency.
Avoid unnecessary features or complexity.
Focus on solving the core desk-booking problem.
Key Principle

"Simplicity is key. Does the job, that's it."
